# v2.11.1 — Visual Storytelling Upgrade

## Scope

This release is a visual-only corrective pass over the accepted v2.11.0 baseline. Gameplay, case canon, five-clue counts, save key (`detektif-bahasa-v1`), scoring, stars, achievements and progression are unchanged.

## C024 educational images

The six former CSS diagrams are replaced by local 768×576 WebP illustrations:

- V01: moon in the night sky — `c024-v01-bulan.webp`
- V02: November calendar — `c024-v02-kalendar.webp`
- V03: reading glasses on an open book — `c024-v03-mata-membaca.webp`
- V04: quiz score of five — `c024-v04-mata-pertandingan.webp`
- V05: feet walking on a garden path — `c024-v05-kaki-berjalan.webp`
- V06: four legs of a wooden table — `c024-v06-kaki-meja.webp`

Before validation, visible captions are deliberately neutral: `Gambar A` through `Gambar F`. Internal identifiers V01–V06 remain unchanged. Meaningful `alt` and `aria-label` descriptions remain available to assistive technology. After validation, the descriptive caption is shown together with its neutral picture letter.

The images were generated as one consistent 3×2 Bayuraya-style contact sheet and then losslessly separated by composition before WebP encoding. The generation prompt requested polished child-friendly 2D educational panels, no recurring characters, equal 4:3 cells, a moon, November calendar, reading glasses/book, five-point quiz board, walking feet and a four-legged table, with no watermark or unrelated text.

## Resolution storytelling

- C021 was preserved. Its existing resolution already places Arissa and Pak Rahim naturally over the Taman Bayuraya scene.
- C022 now uses `balai-resolusi-c022.webp` with the official Puan Suraya and five-year-old Affan poses integrated into the balai environment.
- C023 now uses `perpustakaan-resolusi-c023.webp` with the official Cikgu Farah and Mei Ling poses integrated into the library. Cikgu Farah's canonical hijab remains unchanged and fully covers her hair and neck.
- C024 now uses `dataran-resolusi-c024.webp` with the official Xiao Feng and Ayyash poses integrated into the exhibition scene.

Original hotspot backgrounds are retained and remain the investigation assets. The new scene composites are used only by completed-case resolution screens.

## Implementation

`integratedResolutionPresentation()` is a small shared presentation helper that renders a single responsive scene image with an accessible caption. `visual-storytelling.js` wraps only the three resolution renderers and replaces their prior adjacent environment/pose-card presentation. Investigation dialogue continues to use the established mugshot system.

No remote assets, new dependencies or save-schema changes were introduced.
