# v2.11.0 — C024 Language Validation

## Approach

The child-facing term is **makna perkataan mengikut konteks**. The case deliberately avoids testing technical distinctions between polisemi and homonimi.

## Official references

- PRPM `bulan`: https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?d=74812&keyword=bulan
- PRPM `mata`: https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?d=10456&keyword=mata
- PRPM `kaki`: https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?d=107200&keyword=Kaki
- PRPM `mulut`: https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?keyword=mulut
- PRPM `polisemi`: https://prpm.dbp.gov.my/Cari1?d=175768&keyword=polisemi
- Dewan Bahasa background: https://dewanbahasa.jendeladbp.my/2023/05/05/7904/

## Validation result

The live PRPM pages confirmed:

- `bulan` as the earth's natural satellite and as a named calendar period;
- `mata` as an organ of sight, competition marks/points and the sharp part of a cutting tool.

The `kaki` and `mulut` pages timed out during this implementation pass. Their examples therefore use the editorially locked targets supplied for C024 and Lensa Bahasa. The sentences provide strong contextual support and avoid presenting the examples as a technical lexical classification exercise.

## Child-facing wording

All explanations direct the player to the full sentence, surrounding nouns, actions, time or location. No answer depends on colour alone, a hidden technical term or an unsupported inference.
