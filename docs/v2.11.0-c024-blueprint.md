## Narrative solution

Short exhibition labels omit the surrounding sentence. Visitors therefore connect `bulan`, `mata` and `kaki` to different meanings and pictures. No theft or sabotage occurred. The exhibition is restored by combining each complete sentence with a contextual clue, intended meaning and matching picture.

## Five clues

1. Panduan Pameran: complete sentences use the same word in different contexts.
2. Galeri Gambar: six images show distinct objects or situations.
3. Kad Paparan: shortened labels omit the complete context.
4. Catatan Pengunjung: visitors report conflicting interpretations.
5. Panduan Pengelola: each final explanation requires sentence, context, meaning and picture.

## Evidence and deduction

- EB-01: P1 + P3 prove missing context.
